Wonder Club

Booky Scout

↗

Our weekly scouting report on what is moving in English-language books, in Indonesia and worldwide. We use it to decide what to buy. Resellers use it to decide what to stock.

What it is

One page, every Monday

Booky Scout reads the international bestseller lists, the Indonesian retail and social signals, and our own till data across six stores, then ranks every title by one of six rules. The output is a short list with an action next to each book: deep buy, reorder, investigate, soft buy, monitor, or display.

It is the public edition of the merchandising team's Bestsellers Compass. Internally it drives purchase orders. Externally it goes out as a newsletter that bookshops, schools and online sellers who buy from Sinar Starbooks can forward to their own teams.

For WonderWhat to buy more of this week, by department, before stock-outs.
For resellersThe same signals, one week ahead of the shelf, with a link to order.
For readersWhat people are actually reading, with no publisher copy.
How we get to the conclusions

The method

Every row in the issue can be traced back to these steps. No title is flagged without a source you can check.

01

Two tiers of sources

International lists (Publishers Weekly, NYT, USA Today, Indie Bestsellers, Waterstones, Amazon Charts, a BookTok composite, Goodreads) tell us what global publishers are pushing. Local sources (Periplus, Gramedia, Kinokuniya ID, Tokopedia, Shopee, TikTok Shop, Indonesian press, Bookstagram) tell us what sells here. The local tier decides; the international tier warns early.

02

Our own numbers

Unit sales per title across the six Wonder stores and Sinar Starbooks reseller orders. A title that moved 1.5× its four-week average is "peaking" whatever the charts say.

03

Evergreens first

About 40 perennials (Atomic Habits, The Alchemist, Dog Man, Sapiens, The Psychology of Money and others) are preserved every week. The scout never drops an evergreen for a novelty; it only marks it peaking or quiet.

04

Six rules, in priority order

  1. Cross-channel hit: 3+ international lists and a local source. Deep buy.
  2. Evergreen peaking: a perennial also charting. Reorder before stock-out.
  3. Local-only signal: Indonesia spike, often press or BookTok translation. Investigate.
  4. International cross-list: 3+ lists, no local signal. Soft buy, watch week two.
  5. Single-list #1: NYT or PW only. Monitor.
  6. BookTok or romantasy: younger demand. Physical edition and display priority.
05

A human makes the call

The scout surfaces signals; the buying team places orders. Nothing is purchased automatically, and every "why" line is written by a person in under 14 words of our own, never publisher copy.

06

What we do not claim

No price intelligence, no competitor pricing, no Bahasa-language charts, no POS integration beyond unit sales. When a local source could not be read that week, the issue says so in "Sources read this week".
