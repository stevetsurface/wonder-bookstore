Concept

Everything gets better with books & coffee

Wonder Bookstore is designed as a space where book seekers can explore, discover, and satisfy their curiosity. Rather than simply providing a place to purchase books, the store creates an environment that encourages customers to stay, browse, and discover something beyond what they initially came looking for.

The store's signature green creates a strong sense of identity while bringing warmth, belonging, and cosiness. Combined with carefully considered lighting, the interior is designed to feel comfortable and inviting rather than overly bright or intimidating.

All questions ↗

Do you sell English-language books?

Yes. Our range is imported English-language titles across business, self-development, fiction and classics, fantasy, romance, children's and young readers, manga and graphic novels, bought direct from US and UK publishers.

Can you find a title that isn't on the shelf?

Ask the store team on WhatsApp. They can check the other five stores and the Sinar Starbooks warehouse for you.

Where can I buy online?

Through Wonder's official online shops. Online order support runs through our official contact directory, and every store also sells in person.

Which stores have Wonder Coffee?

Ashta District 8 in SCBD and Berawa in Canggu, Bali. Both are open for a coffee whether or not you are buying a book.

Do you host events or collaborations?

Yes. Author launches, kids' activities, brand collaborations and anniversaries happen across the stores. A separate contact handles Instagram and collaboration enquiries.

What are the opening hours?

Stores open daily during their mall's hours. Berawa is a street-level store and opens daily. Check each store's page for the Maps link.
