Who we are

Welcoming spaces, filled with possibilities

Wonder brings books, gifts, stationery and toys together in welcoming spaces where people of all ages can explore and connect. The first store opened at Hublife in 2022; today there are six, four in Greater Jakarta and two in Bali.

Wonder is the retail home of Sinar Starbooks, an importer and distributor of English-language books, stationery and gifts since 2000, buying direct from leading US and UK publishers. That is why new titles reach our shelves fast and the range runs from business and self-development to fiction, fantasy, children's books, manga and graphic novels.
